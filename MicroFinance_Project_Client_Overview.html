5. Policy & Term Deposit Management (FD, RD, MIS, DDS)
Investment Engine

Industrial deposit plan catalog enabling micro-savings schemes, daily doorstep collections, and high-yield fixed investments.

Deposit Scheme Operational Mechanism Key Features & Controls
Fixed Deposit (FD) One-time lumpsum deposit with compounding tenure interest. Auto-calculated maturity value, bond generation, premature withdrawal penalties.
Recurring Deposit (RD) Monthly fixed installment savings over predetermined months. Late payment penalty calculation, installment passbooks, payment receipts.
Daily Deposit (DDS / Pigmy) Doorstep daily small savings collected via field agents. Batch daily premium entries, agent collection tracking, flexible tenures.
Monthly Income Scheme (MIS) Lumpsum deposit with monthly interest payouts to savings account. Automated monthly interest disbursement register, reinvestment options.
Flexible Premium Plan Variable investment amounts with flexible renewal schedules. Customized interest compounding, transaction slips, and partial liquidation.
•
Bond / Certificate Generation: Print official Investment Bonds and Bond Re-issuance logs.
•
Installment Record Book: Micro-level ledger tracking every renewal installment and penalty waiver.

05 Governance, Audit Safety & Maturity Settlements

9. Dual-Control Maker-Checker Approval System
Zero Fraud Security

Industry-standard four-eye principle ensuring all financial operations and master modifications undergo independent verification.

Approval Queue Workflow Scope Risk Prevention Goal
Customer & KYC Approvals Verifies identity proofs and member eligibility. Prevents ghost accounts and duplicate registrations.
Share & Equity Transactions Validates share transfers and certificate allocations. Ensures strict equity ledger balancing.
Investment Schemes (FD/RD/DD) Authorizes new term deposit booking & rate locks. Avoids unauthorized interest rate offerings.
Savings & Account Transfers Validates high-value deposits, cashouts & fund transfers. Eliminates teller counter discrepancies.
Loan Sanctions & EMI Payments Supervises credit approvals and manual waiver postings. Stops unauthorized disbursement & fine waivers.
Maturity & Inter-Branch Cash Clears final claim payouts and physical cash movements. Guarantees cash vault and fund security.
