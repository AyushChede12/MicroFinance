1. Executive Summary

The proposed MicroFinance Management System (MFMS) is an all-in-one, secure, enterprise-grade banking and credit operations application. Designed specifically for co-operative societies, microfinance institutions (MFIs), and local credit unions, this system automates end-to-end customer onboarding, savings accounts, credit distribution (including Joint Liability Groups and Gold Loans), shareholdings, and commission settlements for agents.

2. Key Modules & Scope

  • Customer Onboarding & KYC Management: Secure customer registration panel, Nominee mapping, smart document uploads (Aadhar, PAN, Voter ID, driving license) and dynamic multiple image uploads.
  • Savings & Current Accounts: Deposit, withdrawal ledger operations, transactional logs, SMS alert toggles, and account status controls.
  • Customer Shareholding Panel: Share capital allocation database, digital certificates log, and share transfer registry.
  • Loan Management Engine (LMS): Dynamic interest rate and payment schemes setup, Loan applications, Gold collateral evaluations (Secured Gold Loan module), and JLG group lending schemes.
  • Financial Consultants & Agents: Agent profiles onboarding, level designations, and automated commission calculations based on collection metrics.
  • System Configurations & Reports: Multi-branch configurations, bank details, company branding configurations, and real-time dashboard ledger reporting.

3. Technology Stack

Backend Framework: Java 17, Spring Boot, Spring Data JPA, Hibernate ORM.
Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, Javascript, jQuery, AJAX.
Database & Server: MySQL 8+ Database, Tomcat 9 Web Server wrapper.
